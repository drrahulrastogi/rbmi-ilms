Public catalogue and temporary cart

Open Browse public catalogue or https://rbmi-ilms.vercel.app/?opac=1 without a staff account.

How to use this section

  1. Select a library and search title, author, ISBN or subject.
  2. Review publisher/year, classification and exact availability totals. Only a small preview of physical copies is shown on each title card.
  3. Use Previous titles / Next titles to browse. Select Browse physical copies, then First copies / Next copies to read accession, shelf and status in pages of 50. Add titles to the temporary cart and print the selection/catalogue page.
  4. Return through Staff / reader sign in. Linked patrons manage their own loans and holds only after authentication.

Notes and limits

Anonymous data contains library names, bibliographic records and limited holdings. It excludes patron details, accounts, prices, fines and audit history.

Cart selections are temporary and reset on refresh. Purchase suggestions, reviews, tags, ratings and anonymous holds are outside this release.
