Password management

Forgotten password

Sign in as administrator. Open Settings → Institution access. Match the user UUID with your private login list, click Reset password, enter your own current password, and enter and confirm the new password. Use 12–128 characters including uppercase, lowercase, a number and a symbol. Verify the account owner first. Share the new password privately and ask them to change it after signing in.

Change your own password

All roles have Change my password in the sidebar beneath Help. Enter your current password and your new password twice, then Save changes.

Administrators must have admin access to every campus assigned to the target account. Existing passwords cannot be viewed. If the administrator forgets their own password, the Supabase project owner must restore access. The original private login CSV does not update after changes.
