Change or recover a password

Email recovery is available to every role, including the administrator, through Supabase Auth and the configured SMTP service.

How to use this section

  1. For a newly imported student or faculty member, sign in using the CSV email and its initial password. A blank password cell uses INSTITUTE@PATRON_CODE. The system requires a different personal password before library records can be accessed; enter the initial/current password, new password and confirmation, save, then sign in again.
  2. When signed in, select Change my password. Enter the current password, a different new password and matching confirmation.
  3. When unable to sign in, choose Forgot password? Recover by email. Enter the registered address and check inbox and spam folders.
  4. Open the newest recovery link. Continue securely if prompted, then enter and save a matching new password. Successful recovery signs you out; sign in with the new password.
  5. Use 12 to 128 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, a number and a symbol. Choose a unique password for each person.
  6. Admin may reset another assigned account in Settings > Institution access after verifying the person's identity and entering the administrator password. Share the temporary password privately and ask the person to change it.
  7. For an expired, incomplete or previously used link, request a new email. Ask admin to correct a wrong registered email.

Notes and limits

Staff cannot reset the administrator; the administrator recovers through their own registered email.

Old passwords cannot be read from Supabase. Passwords are excluded from audit records, GitHub and public source downloads. An initial-password file becomes stale after users change their passwords.

The first password change is enforced by the database, not only the screen. Old sessions cannot access records after setup completes. Forgotten-password recovery remains available by email; a new imported account must still finish its first-login form.
