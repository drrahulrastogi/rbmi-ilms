Operator & administrator manual

Policy settings, holidays & operational controls

Only an admin can save policies, add/update holidays or assign institution memberships. Librarians and other staff may view settings. Each campus has a distinct policy row for each category.

SettingMeaning
loan_daysCalendar days added before moving the due date to an open day.
max_loansMaximum open loans for a patron.
max_renewalsAllowed renewals per loan; zero forbids renewal.
fine_rateINR per eligible overdue day.
grace_daysEligible fine days waived before the rate is applied.
fine_capMaximum assessed fine per returned loan; zero means no fine, not unlimited.
block_balanceBorrowing blocked only when assessed outstanding fines are above this value; equality is allowed.
exclude_weekendsExclude Saturday/Sunday from fine days and move due dates away from those days.

Policies are snapshotted at issue, including renewal limits. Test a new issue after changing a policy. A changed category policy does not rewrite existing loans.

Holidays

Add the campus date/name. Saving the same date updates its name; there is no delete button in this release. To remove an erroneous date, the trusted database administrator deletes that institution/date from app.holidays via SQL Editor, documenting the maintenance externally. Add holidays before issuing/returning whenever possible. All configured holidays are fine-exempt even if weekends are counted.

Day-to-day controls

Use named operator accounts, confirm the campus before bulk upload, review patron-update semantics, and reconcile collection receipts. Do not automatically resubmit a payment after a network timeout; refresh the ledger first to avoid double recording. Use browser refresh/data refresh after another operator changes records.
