Operator & administrator manual

Reports, exports & audit trail

Overview shows total physical copies and titles, current loans, overdue loans and outstanding assessed fines. Collection status summarises copy states. Loans needing attention lists overdue copies.

Reports exports the collection register, circulation history, overdue register, fine balances, patron register and acquisitions. Most borrowed titles counts all historical issues per title. The institution selector limits exports to the selected campus. Patron role has no staff report module; own records remain accessible through Circulation/Holds/Fines.

Tables show the first 30 records with Show more for further records. CSV exports include all loaded records. Nested policy objects in loan exports are serialised as JSON text; authoritative policy snapshots remain in the database/loan history. Exports are data extracts, not a full database backup.

Audit

Every successful data-changing action records campus, Auth actor UUID, action, timestamp and submitted details. View Details shows the JSON payload. Fine waiver reasons, copy status reasons and imports are visible. Failed operations abort without a success audit entry. Authentication events are available separately in Supabase logs.

Application users cannot edit/delete audit records through the client. Database owners retain administrative database access. Bulk imports record both per-row actions and batch details, increasing database usage. There is no automated retention purge in this release; plan retention under institutional policy and back up before any trusted administrative archival.
