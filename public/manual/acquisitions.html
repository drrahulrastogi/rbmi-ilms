Acquisitions, budgets, funds and baskets

Acquisitions track vendors, orders and full receipt of copies. Budget/fund and vendor-basket controls implement the targeted workflows from the supplied Koha manuals.

How to use this section

  1. Add a vendor and the bibliographic title to purchase.
  2. Create a dated budget and allocate funds with unique codes. Total fund allocations cannot exceed the budget.
  3. Create a vendor basket if grouping purchases. Order with vendor, title, invoice, quantity and unit price; optionally select fund and basket.
  4. For a funded order, the budget must be current, basket vendor must match and committed plus received value must fit the fund.
  5. Receive an ordered acquisition once, supplying accession/barcode prefixes. Receipt creates the full ordered quantity atomically.
  6. Review order status, receipt dates and fund commitments. Export orders for accounts reconciliation.

Notes and limits

Unfunded orders are permitted; operationally select the approved fund when budget control is required.

Partial receipt, EDI, foreign-currency conversion, accounting ledgers and hierarchical subfunds are outside this release.
