Notifications and desk updates

In-app borrower notifications

Open Notifications in the top bar. Notifications are scoped to the signed-in borrowing membership in the selected library. They refresh when the workspace changes, when the window regains focus, and every minute while the app is open.

  • Librarian unavailable: borrowers in that library see today's recorded absence. Other libraries do not receive the notice. The availability record also excludes that day from overdue fine calculations.
  • Due soon: only the borrower sees reminders from three days before the due date through the due date. Returning the book clears the reminder.
  • Fine due: only the borrower sees their assessed unpaid balances. Payment or waiver clears the notice when the balance becomes zero. These are assessed balances, rather than predictions of a future fine.

These notices are inside the application. They are not email, SMS or operating-system push notifications, and they are visible when the borrower opens the app.

Account and password search

The administrator can search Accounts and password management by name or email. Search covers all accounts assigned to the chosen library, rather than just the first page. Select the matching person before choosing a password operation. Librarian logins do not gain administrator password-reset powers.

Why a linked patron showed Unknown patron

The former screen tried to resolve linked names from only the first 200 loaded patrons. A linked account outside that page could appear as Unknown patron even though the record existed. Linked names now come directly from the database with each membership page.
