Operator & administrator manual

Welcome & system scope

RBMI Integrated Library Management System is a React application with a PostgreSQL backend. It serves RBCET, RBMI, RBCP, RBCN and RBTTI through separate institution workspaces. This manual covers the delivered release, version 1.0, dated 9 October 2026.

The system includes catalogue and physical-copy management, patrons, circulation, holds, fines, bulk CSV upload, acquisitions, stock verification, reporting and audit. It has no public signup screen. Staff and patron accounts are provisioned by administrators.

Read this first

A yellow sample-data banner means you are using a browser-local demonstration. Six mock roles exercise database permissions, but that role selector is not authentication. The sample books and people are fictional. Data is stored in your browser, isolated from other browsers and institutions' live records. A live deployment uses Supabase Auth and PostgreSQL; the sample banner and role selector disappear.

Default policies are starting points, not a claim about RBMI's existing policy: 14-day loans for most categories, 30 days for faculty; 3 copies for most categories, 10 for faculty; 2 renewals; INR 2 per eligible overdue day; 1 grace day; INR 500 cap; block issue/renew above INR 100 outstanding assessed fines; Saturday and Sunday excluded. Every institution/category is configurable.

Terminology

Bibliographic title
A descriptive record: title, author, ISBN, publisher, subject, DDC/classification and year.
Physical copy
One accession/barcode, shelf, price and circulation state belonging to a title.
Institution membership
An Auth user's role in one campus. A user may hold different roles in different campuses.
Assessed fine
A posted charge created on an overdue return. The dashboard does not estimate unreturned overdue charges.

The date boundary is India time (Asia/Kolkata). This release supports institution-scoped catalogue search; a shared cross-campus OPAC is not included.
