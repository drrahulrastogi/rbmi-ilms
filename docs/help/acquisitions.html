Vendors, orders, invoices & receiving

  1. Create the bibliographic title in Catalogue first.
  2. In Acquisitions, add a vendor with name and optional contact details.
  3. Create a purchase order, selecting vendor/title, invoice or order reference, quantity and unit price.
  4. When the shipment is accepted, choose Receive.
  5. Enter a unique accession prefix and shelf. The app creates PREFIX-1 through PREFIX-N and campus-prefixed barcodes in one transaction.
  6. Print labels from Catalogue and check the received status.

The invoice/order reference is unique per vendor/title in the campus. Multiple titles can share one invoice reference. Received value is quantity multiplied by unit price; tax, discounts and multi-line invoice accounting are not modelled. Receive accepts the full ordered quantity only. Partial receipts are not supported.

A received order cannot be received again. Duplicate accession/barcode failures roll back the copies and preserve ordered status. Cancel applies only to an ordered acquisition. Library records and vendor contacts remain for audit/reporting.

The acquisitions module supports acquisition recordkeeping, not vendor emailing, electronic purchase approval or bank payments.
